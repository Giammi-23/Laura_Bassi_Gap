The Project: from Diagnosis to Structured Knowledge

The starting point of our project is the following macro-question: How does the gender gap reflect itself in the databases that power the Semantic Web? The final goal is to investigate the gender gap within knowledge graphs. We framed this challenge in the historical context of the female professors of the University of Bologna, choosing the figure of Laura Bassi as the central focus of our research. Through a diagnostic audit of the Wikidata graph, we measured the quality and richness of existing data about these scientists, in order to highlight incompleteness and transform fragmented biographical narratives into structured, open, and quantifiable knowledge.

Preliminary Exploratory Queries

Query 1: Census of female scientists at Alma Mater Studiorum

Before proceeding with enrichment and quality assessment, we queried the Wikidata endpoint to map the presence of female professors linked to the University of Bologna. This query aims to isolate potential entities of interest.

SELECT DISTINCT ?person ?personLabel ?personDescription (COUNT(?prop) AS ?numProprietà)
WHERE {
  ?person wdt:P31 wd:Q5 ;         # human being
          wdt:P21 wd:Q6581072 .   # gender: female
  
  {?person wdt:P108 wd:Q131262}     # employer: UNIBO
  
  OPTIONAL { ?person ?prop ?value }
  
  SERVICE wikibase:label { bd:serviceParam wikibase:language "it,en". }
}
GROUP BY ?person ?personLabel ?personDescription
ORDER BY DESC(?numProprietà)
LIMIT 100

We extracted human entities of female gender with the University of Bologna as employer, counting the number of properties associated with them, and limiting and ordering the first 100 results.

Query 2: Identification of the central subject – Laura Bassi

To begin the specific work, we identified our main subject of study: Laura Bassi. We therefore used a label search query (rdfs:label) in Italian to retrieve the unique identifier Q541652.

SELECT DISTINCT ?person ?personLabel ?personDescription
WHERE {
  ?person rdfs:label "Laura Bassi"@it .
  SERVICE wikibase:label { bd:serviceParam wikibase:language "it,en". }

The Four Identified Information Gaps

The comparison between structured Wikidata data and textual sources (Wikipedia and UniBo Historical Archives) revealed four semantic gaps that our project aims to address:

Gap 1: Institutional Status and Historical Primacy

This gap concerns the absence of an explicit property (position held) that attests to Laura Bassi’s role as the first woman in Europe to obtain an official university chair, currently only “implicit” in the graph.

Gap 2: Awards, Honors, and Named Entities

This focuses on the incomplete modeling of numerous honors and dedications (craters, asteroids, ships, and institutions), which, although present in biographical sources, are not properly structured in Wikidata properties.

Gap 3: Invisible Connections (Mentorship and Network)

This explores the lack of structured links between Laura Bassi and her scientific network (such as Alessandro Volta), aiming to surface collaborations and mutual influence often invisible in the graph.

Gap 4: Fields of Activity and Professional Specificity

This addresses the semantic confusion between general disciplines (physics, biology) and specific professional roles (researcher vs professor), aiming to disambiguate the areas in which she operated throughout her career.
