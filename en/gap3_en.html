Gap 3: Invisible Connections (Mentorship and Scientific Networks)

Biographical sources on Laura Bassi reveal a particularly significant network of scientific relationships. Bassi collaborated with Giovanni Battista Beccaria on studies of electricity, maintained contact with Alessandro Volta, and taught her cousin, Lazzaro Spallanzani.

Our objective was therefore to verify whether these relationships were correctly represented within the knowledge graph.

Verification of Academic Relationships

The only result returned is the “student” (P802) relationship with Lazzaro Spallanzani, which is therefore correctly modeled. The other relationships, however, do not appear at all.

SELECT ?person ?personLabel ?property ?propertyLabel WHERE {
  VALUES ?person { wd:Q680 wd:Q981544 wd:Q213351}  # Volta, Beccaria, Spallanzani
  
  wd:Q541652 ?p ?person .
  ?property wikibase:directClaim ?p .

  SERVICE wikibase:label {
    bd:serviceParam wikibase:language "it,en".
  }
}
