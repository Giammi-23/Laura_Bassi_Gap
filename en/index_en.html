A Pioneer of Science

Laura Bassi (Bologna, 1711 - 1778) was an Italian physicist and one of the most important intellectual figures of the European Enlightenment. She was the second woman in Europe to earn a university degree and the first to officially teach at a university.

The University of Bologna boasts a centuries-old tradition of female excellence, with pioneers such as Laura Bassi, the first woman in the world to obtain an official university chair. However, despite their historical importance, these figures often remain "invisible" or poorly structured within major global Knowledge Graphs such as Wikidata.

Through a methodology that combines the rigor of the Semantic Web with the power of Large Language Models (LLMs), our project aims to fill four specific information gaps.
